कैसे इस्तेमाल करें | How to use

Simple steps

  • Cut off a small piece from one end and chew it gently until soft bristles form.
  • Brush your teeth and gums with light strokes, then rinse well.
  • Trim the used end and use a fresh end next time.

Good to know

  • Keep datun dry and clean; use a fresh stick for each person.
  • Brush gently, as with any toothbrush.
  • Each variety differs in taste and firmness; pick what suits you.
